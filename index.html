Mission Control

Herdkit — the engine, in the open

Our multi-agent orchestration engine is open source. This is roughly what it looks like when your project is being built:

A coordinator. A fleet. A gate.

Herdkit runs fleets of AI agents in parallel — each building in an isolated workspace, every change health-checked and adversarially reviewed by other agents, then merged by an autonomous gate. Nothing ships on vibes.

We're working to change how companies use AI: 10× the output at a fraction of the cost — with quality gates a senior engineer would sign off on.
