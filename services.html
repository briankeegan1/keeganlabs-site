1. Compliance Readiness Sprints

Fixed-price packages that get an organization through a specific first-cycle compliance workflow. Each sprint delivers a worked process — not legal advice, not a platform subscription.

California DELETE Act — First-Cycle Sprint

Fixed price · Delivered in days

California's Delete Act (SB 362) requires covered data brokers to process consumer deletion requests through the CPPA's centralized system starting August 2026. First-cycle organizations face a $200/day/request penalty for non-compliance.

This sprint delivers:

  • Field-mapping workbook — your data schema mapped to the required deletion-signal flow
  • Exception matrix — handling partial matches, unverifiable records, processor-held data, and suppression conflicts
  • Processor cascade checklist — downstream vendor mapping and forward-pass documentation
  • First-cycle runbook — step-by-step 45-day cycle guide

Boundary: this sprint delivers a tested operational workflow. It is not legal advice and does not promise a compliance outcome — your counsel owns the legal determination.

EU Cyber Resilience Act — Incident Tabletop

Fixed price · Half-day facilitated drill

The EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) requires manufacturers of products with digital elements to report actively-exploited vulnerabilities within 24 hours and notify authorities within 72 hours — obligations that come into effect September 2026.

This sprint delivers:

  • Scenario template and decision tree for the 24-hour/72-hour windows
  • Evidence checklist for the required notification package
  • Draft notification templates for the relevant national authority
  • 60-minute tabletop facilitation against a synthetic example incident

Boundary: this is an operational drill, not legal advice. Your counsel owns the final legal determinations on notification scope and timing.

EPR Producer-Status Sprint

Fixed price · Delivered in days

Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) packaging regulations — including California SB 54 and analogous state regimes — require covered brand owners and producers to register, determine compliance status, and enroll in a Producer Responsibility Organization (PRO) by statutory deadlines. First-cycle organizations face exposure from missed enrollment windows.

This sprint delivers:

  • Producer-status determination workbook — jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction applicability analysis
  • First-cycle enrollment runbook — PRO selection, reporting, and submission steps
  • Compliance calendar — key deadlines for your product and market footprint

Boundary: this sprint delivers a structured workflow and status determination — not legal advice. Regulatory interpretation is your counsel's domain.
