About

1. Who we are

Keegan Labs is a small, AI-accelerated shop. The principal is Brian Keegan, a software engineer based in Fort Myers, FL, with seven years of experience building data systems in government, defense, and ad-tech. Delivery is augmented by an AI agent fleet — research, drafting, and repeatable data work run through a directed agent pipeline under senior review — which lets a small team turn around engagements at a speed and price point that larger shops cannot.

Small senior team, AI-accelerated. You get direct access to the principal engineer on every engagement. The agent infrastructure handles the commodity work; Brian handles the judgment calls, quality gate, and client relationship.
